A single one-line 1-star review from a houseplant about the human who owns it, under 280 characters:

> ★ One star. My human repotted me into a pot with no drainage, then blamed the soil when I drowned — the soil was fine, the pot was a bathtub with delusions.

`main.py` composes the review from a rating, a grievance and a payoff, gates it to 1–279 characters on a single line, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
